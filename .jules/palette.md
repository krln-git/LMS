## 2024-05-20 - Initial Entry
**Learning:** Foundational accessibility patterns are crucial for Mako templates.
**Action:** Always check for missing ARIA labels in HTML/Mako templates.
## 2024-08-09 - Accessible Icon Buttons in Video Feedback
**Learning:** Icon-only feedback buttons in video components lack accessible names by default.
**Action:** Always add explicit `aria-label` attributes to icon-only interactive elements and hide the inner icons.
