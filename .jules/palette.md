## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Mako Template Buttons
**Learning:** In the edx-platform Mako templates (e.g., `lms/templates/video.html`), there is a pattern of using icon-only buttons (like `thumbs-up-btn` and `thumbs-down-btn` for transcript feedback) without accessible names. The internal `<span>` elements contain the `fa` classes for icons but lack `aria-hidden="true"`, and the parent `<button>` lacks `aria-label` or `title`. This makes the buttons completely opaque to screen reader users, who will just hear "button" without context.
**Action:** When working in edx-platform Mako templates, proactively check icon-only buttons for missing `aria-label` attributes (using `${_('...')}` for translation strings) and ensure the inner icon elements have `aria-hidden="true"`.
