## 2024-04-14 - Add title and data-tooltip to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only social share buttons that lack tooltips and titles are inconsistent and poor for accessibility. Adding `title` and `data-tooltip` ensures screen readers read them correctly and sighted users have hover feedback.
**Action:** Always ensure all icon-only buttons in social sharing or action groups have `title` or `aria-label` along with `data-tooltip` for consistent UX across components.
